Can I use Foceli for meditation?

Yes. Choose a calming world, set your timer and use Sounds or enjoy the quiet. You can spend the session following your breath or your own meditation practice. Completed sessions contribute to your progress and Home rewards, just like other completed focus sessions. Foceli provides a setting for self-directed meditation rather than guided meditation lessons.

Manage your subscription

Use the store account that purchased your membership:

  • Apple subscription settings
  • Google Play subscriptions

Your store shows your renewal date and cancellation options. Eligible yearly plans may include a three-day trial. Cancel before the store's stated deadline if you do not want to be charged. Deleting Foceli does not cancel your subscription.

Sign in and recover your profile

In the installed iPhone app, choose Continue with Apple. Use the same Apple account on later visits or another iPhone to recover your saved username and successfully uploaded progress. You choose your username once after membership is active; a username is not a password or a separate login. Your Apple session stays signed in between visits. The Profile panel includes your Apple account and a Sync saved progress action.

Recovery includes uploaded completed-focus minutes, session counts, daily history and earned decorations. Active sessions, Home customization and other local settings remain on their original device. Progress that has not successfully uploaded cannot be recovered on a different device.

Restore purchases

Choose Restore purchases on Foceli's membership screen using the same store account. Restoration checks subscription access. It does not recover session history or Home progress stored on another device.

Delete your account and data

Open Privacy & account in Settings, the Café menu or the paywall, then choose Delete account. Finish or leave an active session first. Confirm only when you are ready to remove your online account and associated records, including your member profile and cloud recovery history.

Local focus history stays on the device. To remove it, remove the app's stored data using your device's controls; uninstalling, offloading and device backups can behave differently. Cancel your store subscription separately.

If you cannot access the app, email Madsjohannessen03@gmail.com with the subject “Foceli account deletion” and relevant account information, such as the account identifier shown in the app, if available. We may need to verify ownership. An email address alone may not identify a device-created account. We will explain any verification limitations.

Billing questions, refunds and complaints

For a store charge or refund, use the help options in the Apple or Google account used for the purchase. For a problem with Foceli, email us with your device, app version and a description of the issue. You can also contact us about a cancellation or withdrawal request. Include the purchase date and order reference if relevant, but never your full card details. Your statutory consumer rights remain unchanged.

Report a Café problem

Use the Café menu to report or block a participant, or email us with the nickname, approximate session time and what happened. Avoid including unnecessary personal details. You can leave the room at any time.

Parents and guardians

Foceli is intended for ages 13 and over, with guardian permission for minors. Contact us with concerns about a child's account or purchase. Store parental controls can help manage purchases, but account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

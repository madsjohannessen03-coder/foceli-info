Can I use Foceli for meditation?

Yes. Choose a calming world, set your timer and use Sounds or enjoy the quiet. You can spend the session following your breath or your own meditation practice. Completed sessions contribute to your progress and Home rewards, just like other completed focus sessions. Foceli provides a setting for self-directed meditation rather than guided meditation lessons.

Manage your subscription

Use the store account that purchased your membership:

  • Apple subscription settings
  • Google Play subscriptions

Your store shows your renewal date and cancellation options. Eligible yearly plans may include a three-day trial. Cancel before the store's stated deadline if you do not want to be charged. Deleting Foceli does not cancel your subscription.

Restore purchases

Choose Restore purchases on Foceli's membership screen using the same store account. Restoration checks subscription access. It does not recover local focus history or a lost member profile.

Delete your account and data

Open Privacy & account in Settings, the Café menu or the paywall, then choose Delete account. Finish or leave an active session first. Confirm only when you are ready to remove your username, cloud focus history and associated account records.

Local focus history stays on the device. To remove it, remove the app's stored data using your device's controls; uninstalling, offloading and device backups can behave differently. Cancel your store subscription separately.

If you cannot access the app, email Madsjohannessen03@gmail.com with the subject “Foceli account deletion” and your username or relevant account information. We may need to verify ownership. An email address alone may not identify a device-created account. We will explain any verification limitations.

Report a Café problem

Use the Café menu to report or block a participant, or email us with the nickname, approximate session time and what happened. Avoid including unnecessary personal details. You can leave the room at any time.

Parents and guardians

Foceli is intended for ages 13 and over, with guardian permission for minors. Contact us with concerns about a child's account or purchase. Store parental controls can help manage purchases, but account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Foceli is operated by Mads Johannessen, the controller responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: Madsjohannessen03@gmail.com.

Foceli can be used for work, study and self-directed meditation. This policy applies to each of these uses. Meditation sessions use the same timer and progress records described below.

What stays on your device

Foceli stores your detailed focus history, active timer, earned rewards, Home customization and preferences locally. Any saved project names, intentions and earlier onboarding answers also remain local in the current implementation. Your device or operating-system backup settings may affect these files.

Your member profile

When you create a member profile, we store a generated account ID, your chosen username and its creation time with Supabase. We also upload a random installation identifier, your total completed-focus time, monthly completed-focus totals and sync timestamps. This includes existing local totals when your profile is created. These totals are private; Foceli does not offer a public leaderboard or member directory.

We do not upload project names, intentions or your daily focus breakdown through profile sync. Your username is not a login credential. The current device-held identity does not provide account recovery or cloud-to-device restoration after a reinstall.

Quiet Café

Using a Café creates a generated account identity if needed. We process room and participant IDs, your shared nickname, readiness and participation status, session duration, selected shared soundtrack, server timestamps and invitation information to run the room. Other participants can see the nickname and participation information you share at their table. Choose a nickname that does not reveal private information and share invitations only with people you intend to invite.

Reports and blocks contain the relevant account IDs, report category and associated moderation information, which can include a reported nickname. These help protect participants. Foceli does not provide chat, voice calls or photo sharing in the Café, and does not need microphone or camera access for this feature.

Subscriptions

Apple or Google handles your payment details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, transaction and product information, subscription status and an app user identifier to validate purchases, restore access and provide subscription reporting. When your identity is connected, the identifier links your membership to your Supabase account. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

Support and technical information

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive information. Our infrastructure providers process connection and security information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and protect their services.

These information pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website connection and security information under its privacy statement.

Why we use information

  • To provide the features you request, maintain your profile and validate membership: performance of our agreement with you.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, handle reports and maintain essential operational records: our legitimate interests in a safe, reliable service, balanced against your rights.
  • To answer support requests: performance of our agreement or our legitimate interest in helping users, depending on the request.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. If a future optional feature requires consent, we will request it separately and explain how to withdraw it.

Providers and international processing

Supabase hosts the app database in Frankfurt, EU. RevenueCat supports subscription management; Apple and Google operate their respective stores. Gmail handles email sent to our support address. Providers may process service, support or technical data outside your country, including outside the EEA. An EU database location does not mean every provider operation occurs in the EU.

Provider agreements describe safeguards for international processing, including standard contractual clauses where applicable: Supabase data processing addendum and RevenueCat data processing addendum. Contact us for information or a copy of the relevant safeguards. Provider details: Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple and Google.

How long information is kept

  • Local progress remains on your device until the app's stored data is removed; device backups may retain copies.
  • Member profiles and private focus aggregates are kept until account deletion. Expired membership does not erase earned progress or the profile.
  • Unstarted Café rooms expire after 30 minutes. Started rooms expire approximately 24 hours after their scheduled finish. An hourly cleanup removes expired records.
  • Café rate-limit records are removed after one day and moderation reports after 30 days, through the hourly cleanup.
  • Inactive anonymous guests are eligible for removal after 30 days when they have no room membership, host record or member profile. Host and member identities are retained until deletion.
  • Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and related disputes or legal obligations. Provider logs, billing records and backups have separate retention rules; deleting an application record does not instantly erase every backup or store transaction.

Deletion and your rights

You can request account deletion from Privacy & account in the app, including from Settings or the paywall. Finish or leave an active session first. Deletion removes your account, username, cloud focus history and associated Café records, and requests removal of the linked RevenueCat customer record when connected. A running room can remain available to other participants after its host leaves or deletes their account.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription and does not erase local focus history. Cancel your subscription through the store separately. Creating another profile can upload the local history again. See account deletion help.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email us to exercise these rights; we may need proportionate information to verify the request. If you lose the device-held identity, we may be unable to link you safely to an old account. We normally respond to privacy-rights requests within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we will explain it. You can complain to your local supervisory authority, including Datatilsynet in Norway.

Age and parents

Foceli is intended for people aged 13 and over. Minors need a parent or legal guardian's permission to use the service and make purchases. Local rules may require additional parental authorization. Foceli is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion.

Changes

We will update this policy when the service or data practices change. Significant changes will be brought to your attention in the app or through another appropriate notice before they take effect where required.
